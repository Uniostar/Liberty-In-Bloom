🌸 Our Nation’s Founding Document

The Declaration of Independence

On July 4, 1776, delegates from thirteen British colonies in North America adopted the Declaration of Independence, formally announcing their separation from Great Britain and establishing the United States of America as a sovereign nation. Drafted primarily by Thomas Jefferson, the document drew on Enlightenment philosophy to articulate the fundamental belief that all men are created equal and endowed with certain unalienable rights. Its bold assertion that governments derive their just powers from the consent of the governed inspired revolutions across the globe and continues to resonate as a beacon of freedom and human dignity nearly 250 years later.

Founding Fathers

TJ

Thomas Jefferson

Primary Author of the Declaration

A Virginia planter, lawyer, and polymath, Thomas Jefferson was chosen at age 33 to draft the Declaration of Independence. His eloquent prose gave voice to the colonists' grievances and established the philosophical foundations of American democracy. Jefferson later served as the third President of the United States and founded the University of Virginia.

JA

John Adams

Delegate & Champion of Independence

Known as the "Atlas of Independence," John Adams of Massachusetts was instrumental in persuading the Continental Congress to vote for independence. A passionate advocate for colonial rights since the Stamp Act crisis, Adams later became the first Vice President and second President of the United States.

BF

Benjamin Franklin

Statesman, Scientist & Diplomat

At 70, Benjamin Franklin was the eldest signer of the Declaration of Independence. A celebrated inventor, author, and diplomat, Franklin served as America's representative in France, securing the crucial alliance that helped win the Revolutionary War. His legendary wit and wisdom made him one of the most recognized Americans of his era.

🌸 Our Purpose

Why Liberty in Bloom?

Liberty in Bloom was created to honor the extraordinary courage and vision of the Founding Fathers as we approach America’s 250th birthday on July 4, 2026. Just as flowers bloom and reach toward the light, the ideals enshrined in the Declaration of Independence have blossomed across generations, inspiring freedom movements around the world.

🌸 A Living Document

The Declaration of Independence is not merely a historical artifact gathering dust in the National Archives — it is a living promise to every American. Its words “all men are created equal” and “unalienable rights” have fueled abolitionism, women’s suffrage, civil rights, and countless movements for justice and dignity. As we celebrate 250 years, we renew that promise for the generations yet to come.

🌿 Education Through Beauty

We believe history should be beautiful, engaging, and alive. By weaving the story of American independence through the metaphor of flowering and bloom, we invite visitors of all ages to connect emotionally with the sacrifices and triumphs of 1776.
